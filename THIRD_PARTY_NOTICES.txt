Review result: no confirmed redistribution prohibition was identified for the
observed bundled runtime classes. Existing binaries are eligible for experimental
preservation with these companion notices. This is a source-backed engineering
review, not comprehensive legal clearance or a formal dependency/SBOM certification.
Exact build provenance remains in manifests; not every transitive source revision
is independently attested. The earlier M4 incomplete-inventory note was a pending
review, not evidence of a prohibition. The preservation command does not require
finished M4, an installer, signing or Windows 11 acceptance.
